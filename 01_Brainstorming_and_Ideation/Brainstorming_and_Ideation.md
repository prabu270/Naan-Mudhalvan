# Phase 1: Brainstorming and Ideation

## Project Title

**LegalEase: AI-Powered Legal Document Generator**

## 1. Problem Statement

Creating legal documents manually can be time-consuming and difficult for people who are unfamiliar with legal language. Many users struggle to prepare professional legal documents because they lack knowledge of legal formats and terminology.

## 2. Proposed Solution

LegalEase is an AI-powered application that helps users generate structured legal documents by entering simple details. It uses Artificial Intelligence to create draft documents in a professional format.

## 3. Objectives

* Simplify the process of creating legal documents.
* Reduce the time required to prepare document drafts.
* Provide an easy-to-use interface.
* Generate editable documents.
* Allow users to download documents in different formats.

## 4. Target Users

* Students
* Small business owners
* Individuals
* Startups
* People who need basic legal document drafts

## 5. Key Features

* AI-powered document generation
* Simple user interface
* User-provided document details
* Editable document preview
* Download options for TXT, DOCX, and PDF
* Backend integration using FastAPI
* AI integration using Google Gemini

## 6. Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide a simple and accessible platform for generating legal document drafts. It helps users prepare documents more efficiently while reminding them to review the generated content before official use.

## 7. Conclusion

LegalEase combines Artificial Intelligence and web technologies to simplify legal document preparation. It demonstrates how AI can assist users in creating structured documents through an easy-to-use application.

**Note:** LegalEase generates draft documents and does not replace advice from a qualified legal professional.
